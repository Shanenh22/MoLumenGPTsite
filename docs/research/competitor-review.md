## Common visitor needs observed

- Know what kind of reading fits the present question.
- See price, duration, format, preparation, and deliverables before booking.
- Understand whether exact birth time is required.
- Decide whether an astrologer’s approach feels ethical and personally compatible.
- Find educational material that explains terminology without forcing a purchase.

## Differentiation opportunity

Mo can credibly own a “warm study” position: a science teacher’s clarity, a classical lens, candid personal voice, and explicit client agency. The strongest experience is not more mystical decoration; it is calm orientation, transparent choices, and substantive explanations.

## Patterns not to adopt

- Large undifferentiated service menus.
- Claims of accuracy, destiny, guaranteed transformation, or therapeutic results.
- Per-minute chat mechanics and false urgency.
- Generic zodiac-content volume disconnected from Mo’s point of view.
- Heavy embeds, auto-playing video, or visual effects that obscure reading and booking.
