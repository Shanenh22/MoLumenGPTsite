# SEO, GEO, and answer-engine opportunities

## Baseline problems

- Indexable template copies and irrelevant archive categories dilute topical focus.
- Service pages are absent, prices are hidden, and the booking page offers almost no crawlable context.
- Several titles are generic (`General 6`, `Sales Page`, duplicated brand title).
- Current Sky articles stop in February 2025 and need clear dates/archive semantics rather than evergreen “current” framing.
- Internal links in Mo1 point to routes that do not exist on the live domain.

## Priority clusters

1. **Choose and prepare for a reading:** readings overview, verified service pages, comparison, Reading Finder, how readings work, preparation, birth-time guidance, FAQs, booking policy.
2. **Birth chart fundamentals:** chart basics → planets → signs → houses → aspects → glossary.
3. **Timing and transitions:** transits → Current Sky → dated events/articles → archive.
4. **Relationships and purpose:** educational pages connected to only the verified relevant services.
5. **Trust:** About Mo → Approach → Credentials → Disclaimer → Privacy.

## Page-quality rules

- One distinct search intent and one clear H1 per indexable page.
- Answer the primary question early, then provide Mo’s interpretive perspective.
- Use unique titles/descriptions, canonical URLs, breadcrumbs, visible authorship/dates, meaningful internal links, and accurate social metadata.
- Structured data must match visible content. Use Organization/Person/WebSite/WebPage/Breadcrumb/Article/Service selectively; do not add review, rating, course, FAQ, or video markup without eligibility and visible supporting content.
- No separate indexable page for every video and no thin zodiac/keyword variants.

## Technical priorities

- Static Astro output; sitemap, robots, RSS, canonical normalization, redirect inventory, custom 404.
- De-index or redirect copy/template routes after mapping content.
- Responsive images with fixed dimensions; facade embeds; minimal JavaScript.
- Validate against Google’s current good thresholds: LCP ≤2.5 s, INP <200 ms, CLS <0.1 at the 75th percentile.
- Search Console and Bing setup after staging content is approved; do not submit staging for indexing.

## Measurement without sensitive data

Centralize GA4 and allow the site to run with no ID. Event parameters may include service slug, tool step, content category, and placement—but never names, contact details, birth data, location, or free-text questions.
