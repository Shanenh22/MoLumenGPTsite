# Content inventory

Accessed: 2026-08-02. Scope: publicly reachable pages in the live Squarespace sitemap, crawlable navigation, and the Mo1 reference homepage. See `url-inventory.md` for the URL-level record.

## Executive findings

- The live sitemap exposes 55 URLs, including 6 articles, 23 tag/category archives, core pages, duplicate template pages, funnel/template remnants, and utility pages.
- The effective primary navigation is Home, About, Services, Appointments, Blog, Contact, and Book Now.
- The live service copy names four consultation families: Natal Chart, Solar Return, Life Changes, and Relationship. Only the natal duration (90 minutes) is stated. No prices are published.
- The appointments page embeds an Acuity URL carrying a `squarespace-example` parameter; the resolved experience identifies itself as “Booking Demo,” so booking must be considered broken or unverified.
- The Mo1 reference is a single, polished homepage prototype. Most of its navigation points to unbuilt `molumen.com` routes. It proposes Natal, Transit, Relationship, Horary, Further Out in the Sky, and Gift offerings. These are not all confirmed by current live content.
- The live site contains serious launch risks: duplicate/copy pages, unrelated template categories, placeholder legal copy, generic template sales pages, no transparent pricing, and inconsistent paths (`/terms` and `/terms-1`).

## Repetition, contradictions, and stale material

1. Navigation and newsletter/footer text repeat multiple times in extracted page content.
2. `/terms` and `/terms-1` conflict; `/terms-1` contains lorem ipsum and a third-party template promotion.
3. Copy/template URLs are indexable, including copied privacy, terms, newsletter, Instagram, 404, and site-credit pages.
4. Blog categories such as Writing Tips, Freelance, Copywriting, and Entrepreneurship appear unrelated to the astrology practice and likely came from a template.
5. Live homepage promotes “Focus on Where,” while Services names Solar Return and Life Changes; the exact current product catalog is unclear.
6. Live site says “There is fate and there is choice” and sometimes uses predictive language; the rebuild should retain nuance while removing guaranteed or fear-based implications.
7. The footer copyright is 2023 while the newest surfaced article is dated 2025.
8. The privacy policy references Squarespace-specific processing and forum/chat features that may not exist.

## Valuable material to preserve and reorganize

- “See yourself in a new light” is the strongest existing brand line.
- Mo describes herself as practical and inspiring, candid and compassionate, intuitive and skillful, with a classical lens.
- The “mirror or planner, not a belief system” explanation is distinctive and beginner-friendly.
- The sequence “knowledge → insight → power → freedom” captures the agency-centered philosophy, though wording should be reviewed for claims.
- Biography themes—science teaching, business experience, creativity, family, nature, learning, travel—make the practice personal rather than generic.
- The lunation articles show a recognizable pattern: lived metaphor, candid reflection, technical sky context, and practical invitation.

## Missing content with high visitor value

- Plain-language service comparison with prices, duration, format, required birth data, deliverables, and policies.
- What readings can and cannot do; how to prepare; unknown birth time guidance; online-session logistics.
- Ethical astrology and client agency.
- Beginner learning path covering planets, signs, houses, aspects, and transits.
- Accessible booking/privacy explanations before birth data is requested.
- Current Sky freshness model with verification dates and archive behavior.

## Owner-review flags

- All service names and availability, especially Horary, Transit, Further Out in the Sky, relocation/astrocartography, Gift, and Solar Return.
- Every price, duration, format, deliverable, intake requirement, and policy.
- Credential and membership status as of launch.
- Any testimonial and its publication permission.
- Rights and original files for photographs.
- Whether the six existing lunation articles may be migrated and edited.
